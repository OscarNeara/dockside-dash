How to play

Swipe on a phone or tablet. On a computer, use the keys.

  1. Change lane

    Swipe left or right to move between the three lanes.

  2. Jump

    Swipe up to hop over hatboxes and brooks.

  3. Throw a cup

    Swipe down to throw to the best customer in reach, or tap a customer to choose them.

  4. Brew more cups

    Collect tea leaves to brew fresh cups. A bump spills a cup, so keep your basket full!

Where customers wait

  1. Doorways: run in one of the two lanes nearest the houses.
  2. Windows: you can reach them from any lane.
  3. Balconies: jump first, then throw while you are in the air.

A gold ring means you can reach them. Green means a Perfect throw.

On a computer: ←→ change lane ↑ or Space jump ↓ or E throw Esc pause

Add it to your Home Screen

Dockside Dash then opens like an app, full screen, and works offline.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Then open Dockside Dash from your Home Screen.

On iPhone, Share is at the bottom of the screen. On iPad, it is at the top.

Install from your browser

Dockside Dash then opens like an app, full screen, and works offline.

  1. Tap the menu button at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install. Then open Dockside Dash from your home screen.

Open on your phone

Point your phone's camera at the code. Then tap Get the app on your phone to add it to your home screen.
